Part 2: Notification Service Architecture

10M+ notifications/day • Distributed Idempotency • Multi-Channel • Resilient Failovers

Key Architecture Guarantees

  • Throughput Math: 10M/day (~115 req/sec avg, peaking at 5,000–10,000 req/sec during campaigns).
  • Priority Queues: Dual Kafka topics (Transactional high-priority vs Bulk marketing low-priority).
  • Deduplication & Idempotency: Redis distributed locks with 24-hour idempotent deduplication windows.
  • Vendor Resiliency: Circuit breakers & automatic fallbacks (SendGrid → AWS SES, Twilio → MessageBird).
  • User Controls: Preference resolver, per-channel opt-outs, and localized quiet hours enforcement.

Detailed PostgreSQL schemas, event schemas, queue consumer topologies, and Mermaid diagrams are fully documented in SYSTEM_DESIGN.md.
